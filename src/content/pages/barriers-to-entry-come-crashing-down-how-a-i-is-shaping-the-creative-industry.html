to conclude

Recent developments with AI generated image, video and 3D assets from companies like Stable Diffusion, MidJourney and Unreal Engine have shown impressive grounds covered in relatively short periods of time. Further advances are progressing at a telescopic rate, with new and exiting uses of the technology appearing daily. I think it's safe to say at the moment most creative jobs will be aided rather than replaced in the foreseeable future. I don't doubt for a second that AI will not only be with us for a long to come but will also impress and surpass us in many creative fields.
