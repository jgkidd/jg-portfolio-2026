Bud: a little help for your house plants

A personal exploration of a connected plant monitor and companion app, designed to make everyday plant care easier to understand and manage.

The idea

The concept pairs a small monitor with an app to bring water, pH and sunlight information together with plant profiles and care schedules.

Design goals

I explored how to give plant owners a useful overview of their collection, make care tasks easy to find, and keep the experience simple. The hardware brief considered the balance between useful sensors, production cost, battery life and easy charging.

The app

The interface explores four areas: a plant collection, a care schedule, discovery and a profile.

Project status

Bud is a personal design concept. The screens illustrate the proposed experience; sensor performance and usability would need validation through prototyping and testing.
