I’m Josh Griffey

I’m a London-based Senior Product & Interaction Designer with over 21 years crafting digital products, with a particular interest in how software and physical devices work together.

I turn complex user stories into intuitive experiences, using rapid prototypes on real devices to challenge assumptions and refine interactions. My work spans healthcare, entertainment, charity, sport and travel.

Today, I use AI-assisted development to bring designs onto real devices. I direct specialised agents, define core screens and design-system foundations, and review implementation for consistency, readability and unnecessary complexity. I retain responsibility for design and code quality, seeking engineering input when work falls outside my knowledge.

Since January 2026, I’ve been designing and building a sailing app to help people navigate qualifications, log experience and find opportunities to get on the water. It is currently in development.

I’m looking to join a lean startup, contributing across design, prototyping and frontend development.

When I’m not working, you can find me pursuing my other passions, from exploring the great outdoors through sailing, skiing, skateboarding and rock climbing.
