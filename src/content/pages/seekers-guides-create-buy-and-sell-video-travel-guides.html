the brief

I founded and led a team of four to create Seekers Guides, a platform where travel creators could turn their videos into paid guides by linking moments on screen to places, articles and practical information, giving their audiences the details they craved about each trip.

Deliverables:

  • Brand identity and tone of voice
  • UX flows and wireframes
  • App views, components and prototypes
  • Social channels and content strategy
  • Commissioned videos for selected coastal businesses
  • Campaign concepts
  • Creator onboarding and supporting materials

from Coastal to Seekers Guides

Coastal Seekers began with my love of the ocean and a concern about plastic pollution. I wanted to build a platform that could encourage more awareness and reduce personal plastic use. That ambition remained as the community grew beyond sailing and coastal travel creators, with Seekers Guides evolving to reflect a wider range of journeys and audiences.

Guides for everyone

Guides were organised around five types of travel experience, helping people find inspiration that matched their interests. These categories remained as Coastal evolved into Seekers Guides, with Cruiser staying prominent as a nod to the platform’s sailing roots.

turning content into income

By giving travel creators another way to earn from their content, we gave them a reason to share their guides beneath their videos. Their audiences could follow those links to the platform, helping build a community around the creators we called seekers. We took an 8% fee on guide sales, leaving creators with the majority of the income.

from a moment to the detail

A restaurant glimpsed in a travel video could become somewhere a viewer wanted to eat. Our platform let creators link moments in their videos to real information, including places and articles, making that detail part of a guide their audience could buy.

telling coastal businesses’ stories

Along our coastal journey, we selected businesses offering standout experiences. We worked with them to identify their audience, then produced commissioned videos centred on a real person experiencing what they offered. Together, the four videos attracted 51k views.
