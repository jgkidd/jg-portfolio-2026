the brief

I was hired to lead the digital work for HNDSM, a streetwear clothing brand. My role covered the website, video, social content and strategy, bringing a consistent visual direction to the brand’s online presence and creating content to showcase the clothing and connect with its audience.

Deliverables:

  • Website design and development
  • Video production and art direction
  • Social content and channel strategy
  • Content planning and campaign direction
  • Digital direction aligned with the brand’s business goals
